## XbotGo

1. Nail the scan on first setup. Don't move the camera while it boots; bring it back to centre using the tripod. Getting this right stops it overshooting the corners and pulling the stands into frame.
2. Ideal placement is about 20 to 25 feet back and about 10 feet above the glass. That's rarely possible, so be creative and adapt.
3. In Recording settings > Camera motion > Camera rotation angle, adjust the angle slightly. 89 degrees has worked well across several rinks and keeps the ice in frame rather than the stands.
4. Depending on camera height, tilt the camera slightly down. This makes a significant difference.
5. Record at 1080p 60fps. It's very smooth to watch, especially on a large TV.
